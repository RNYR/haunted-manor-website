# Haunted Manor Bot — Terms of Service

**Last updated:** October 15, 2026  
**Operator:** RNYR  
**Contact:** `v4rdr` on Discord

## 1. Purpose and access

Haunted Manor Bot (the **Bot**) is a free, non-commercial Discord application created for a **single private murder mystery game among approximately 20 friends**. It is not offered for installation or use by the general public. Use is limited to invited participants in our private Discord server.

## 2. What the Bot does

Players can use `/roll` to obtain three randomly assigned character traits and `/mytraits` to retrieve their existing assignment. The Bot attempts to ensure one roll per Discord account and no reuse of a trait among players in the game. Results appear as **ephemeral Discord interaction messages**, visible to the requesting player rather than posted to the channel.

## 3. Game rules

Trait assignments are random and do not grant any guaranteed character role or advantage. The organizer may fix errors, reset assignments, modify the trait pool, or end the game. Re-rolling is not normally permitted.

## 4. Respectful use

Use the Bot in accordance with [Discord's Terms](https://discord.com/terms). Do not disrupt it, attempt to access other players' private assignments, or try to extract its stored data.

## 5. Privacy

The Bot stores Discord user identifiers and assigned traits to support game mechanics. Other players do not see ephemeral responses in the channel, but the operator and authorized hosting administrators may be able to inspect stored assignments. See [Privacy Information](privacy.html).

## 6. Availability
